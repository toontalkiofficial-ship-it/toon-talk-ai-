# Privacy Policy — Toon Talk AI

**Status:** Draft. The app publisher must review this policy, add a contact email, and publish it at a stable public URL before distributing the app.

**Last updated:** 4 October 2026

## Information the app handles

- Text prompts you enter are sent to Pollinations to generate images or videos.
- When you connect your Pollinations account, the app receives an access key after you approve the sign-in flow. The key is stored in the app's private on-device preferences so you do not have to connect every time. Android app-data backup is disabled.
- Generated media is downloaded to the app's temporary cache for preview and is saved to your device only when you choose Save result to Gallery.
- This app does not currently include analytics or production advertising SDKs.

## Third-party service

AI generation and account authorization are provided by Pollinations. Your prompts and authorized access key are sent to Pollinations to fulfill generation requests. Pollinations' own terms and privacy practices also apply. Review https://pollinations.ai/ and https://enter.pollinations.ai/ for current information.

## Data sharing and retention

The app publisher does not operate a separate server and does not receive prompts or access keys through a publisher-operated backend. Pollinations processes requests directly. The app stores the access key locally until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Generated files remain in your Gallery until you delete them.

## Security

Do not share your access key. You can revoke Pollinations access from your Pollinations account. No software can guarantee absolute security, and device-level compromise may expose locally stored data.
